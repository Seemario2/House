Trust & Safety

Your security is our top priority. Learn how we keep you safe.

For Tenants

We implement rigorous checks to ensure every listing on our platform is legitimate and every landlord is verified.

  • Verified Landlords: We check ID and ownership documents.
  • Secure Payments: Never pay cash. Use our secure platform.
  • Scam Protection: AI-driven fraud detection systems.
  • Virtual Tours: Verify properties remotely before visiting.

For Landlords

Protect your property and investment with our comprehensive tenant screening and insurance options.

  • Tenant Screening: Credit checks, background checks, and eviction history.
  • Rent Guarantee: Options to ensure you get paid even if tenants default.
  • Damage Protection: Security deposit handling and insurance.
  • Legal Support: Access to standard lease agreements and legal notices.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you encounter anything suspicious or believe you have been the target of a scam, please report it immediately to our safety team at safety@oluwanjobahomes.com or call our 24/7 hotline.
